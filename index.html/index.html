Concepción, Chile

View of Concepción, Chile

Concepción is one of the most important cities in southern Chile and is known for its universities, culture, and beautiful natural surroundings. The city is located near the Pacific Ocean and is surrounded by rivers, forests, and hills. Concepción is recognized as an educational and cultural center and offers many outdoor activities and places to explore.
